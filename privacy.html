Game Library - Privacy Policy

Game Library respects your privacy.

Information Collected

This app does NOT collect personal information.

The app may use your public wallet address only to process transactions.

Local Data

Game progress, unlocked levels, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Blockchain Transactions

Payments are processed via the Solana blockchain. Transaction data is public on the blockchain but is not stored by this app.

Data Sharing

No personal data is sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties.

Security

This app never accesses or stores private keys or sensitive wallet data.
